QUESTIONS

Questions clients often ask.

Straight answers about where we build, approvals, costs and how to get started.

Where is Mara Infra based, and where do you build?

Our office is at 250, Thirugnana Sambandar Street, Srinivasa Nagar, Madipakkam, Chennai 600091. We build across Chennai, and our past projects also include work in Kumbakonam and Tiruvannamalai District.

Do you take care of building approvals?

Yes. We handle CMDA/DTCP building-plan approvals, Panchayat/Municipality permissions, and water and electricity approvals, including all the follow-ups.

What kinds of projects do you build?

Custom homes, villas and apartment complexes; commercial buildings such as schools, offices, retail outlets, warehouses and industrial units; renovation and remodeling; and sustainable buildings with solar and rainwater integration.

How much will my construction cost?

Every project is priced on its own plans, site and materials, so we don't publish fixed rates. After a site visit and consultation, we give you a free, no-obligation estimate.

How do I get started?

Call +91 99621 16999, message us on WhatsApp, or send the enquiry form. Our team gets back to you, visits your site, and then sends your free estimate.

Who leads Mara Infra?

Co-founders Mr. Madhan Kumar, who brings experience from Appasamy Builders, and Mr. Ravichandran, who brings expertise from Mark Builders. Together they have over 20 years in construction and 100+ completed projects.
