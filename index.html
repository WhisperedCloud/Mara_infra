1. Products We Supply

Authorised Wholesale & Retail Distribution
  • Waterproofing Chemicals Elastomeric coatings, PU injection resins, crystalline slurries, and tank sealants providing durable, long-lasting moisture protection.
  • Tile & Stone Adhesives Polymer-modified cement mortars for vitrified tiles, granite, and tile fixing, ensuring superior bond strength and non-slip performance.

2. Contracting Services

Certified Application & Installation Services
  • Waterproofing Contracting Turnkey roof terrace coating, basement leak injection, and tank waterproofing delivered with precision by certified professionals to guarantee permanent leak-free protection.
