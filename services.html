Technical Services & Contracting Capabilities

Beyond product supply, Mara Infra Solutions provides certified contracting, application, and technical inspection services.

CONTRACTING SERVICE 01

Waterproofing Contracting & Leak Control

We execute turnkey waterproofing projects for roof terraces, commercial basements, water tanks, and swimming pools. Our team utilizes 2-component Weber elastomeric slurry membranes and high-pressure polyurethane injection grouts to seal active water leaks.

  • Terrace & Balcony Elastomeric Membrane Coating
  • High-Pressure PU Injection Grouting for Basement Leaks
  • Swimming Pool & Potable Water Tank Lining
  • 10-Year Written Leak-Proof Warranty Certificate
CONTRACTING SERVICE 02

Precision Tile Laying & Epoxy Grouting Application

Our factory-trained application specialists ensure 100% full-bed tile adhesive coverage using notched trowels to prevent tile debonding. We apply Weber Color Epoxy Grout for 100% stain-proof, waterproof tile joints in kitchens, bathrooms, and commercial lobbies.

  • Large Format Vitrified Tile & Slab Installation
  • Vertical Granite & Natural Stone Cladding
  • 100% Stain-Proof Weber Epoxy Grout Application
  • Tile-on-Tile Renovation without Chipping Existing Floors
CONTRACTING SERVICE 03

Saint-Gobain Architectural Glazing & Door Fitting

We supply and install Saint-Gobain toughened safety glass, acoustic office partitions, solar control double glazing (DGU), and heavy-duty UPVC window and door systems for residential villas and commercial complexes.

  • Commercial Structural Glass Facades & Curtain Walls
  • UPVC & Aluminium Door / Window Framing
  • Acoustic Noise-Reduction Partitions & Shower Enclosures
  • Fire-Rated & Toughened Safety Glass Installation
TECHNICAL SERVICE 04

On-Site Technical Support & Moisture Testing

Before specifying any adhesive or chemical, our technical engineers conduct an on-site evaluation using digital moisture meters and infrared diagnostics. We provide written technical specifications and on-site training for contractors.

  • Substrate Porosity & Moisture Meter Testing
  • Written Material Specification Reports for Architects
  • On-Site Application Training for Masonry Teams
  • Pre-Tiling & Pre-Waterproofing Diagnostic Audits
