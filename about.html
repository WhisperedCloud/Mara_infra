OUR IDENTITY

Mara Infra Construction Company

Led by Mr. Madhan Kumar and Mr. Ravichandran, seasoned professionals with over 20 years of experience in the construction industry, Mara Infra has successfully completed 100+ projects over the years, largely through word of mouth. The company is committed to delivering quality homes with precision and care.

Mr. Madhan Kumar brings valuable expertise from his previous role at Appasamy Builders, while Mr. Ravichandran’s experience with Mark Builders adds to the company’s strong foundation in construction excellence.

At Mara Infra, we are passionate about transforming your dream home into reality. With a focus on quality, transparency and customer satisfaction, we offer hassle-free, top-notch construction services you can trust.

Seamless Approvals & Clearances

We manage CMDA/DTCP building-plan approvals, Panchayat/Municipality permissions, water and electricity approvals, plus the follow-ups—giving homeowners a smooth, worry-free experience from start to finish.
